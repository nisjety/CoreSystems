DEFECTS THE PROOF FOUND (neither was visible to any unit or contract test; both fixed in e22befde)
  1. session-core DecideApproval answered 502 for the first decide of a loop approval. Loop approvals have no delivery-outbox row by design and the handler read "granted without a delivery id" as a lost delivery. The write now reports loop_owned and the guard no longer fires for it.
  2. model-gateway /continue answered 503 continue_unavailable. The pre-consume went through the orchestration client, which targets the Go orchestrator-core; that service proxies GetApproval and DecideApproval but does not serve ConsumeApproval (Unimplemented). A separate consume client now targets session-core.
  Side effect of 1: the BFF retried the decide once, and the retry of an already granted approval answered 503 approval_delivery_unknown (the expected answer for a retry); the user-visible effect was only the first-click error.
