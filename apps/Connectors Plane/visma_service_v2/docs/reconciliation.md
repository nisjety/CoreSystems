## `contacts` vs `dm_contacts`: erp_hides

- **25,055 live DataMart rows ERP REST does not return.** Sample keys: 115867, 116010, 116011, 116012, 116013
  - separates them from the shared rows: `lastName = null` on 25,055 of 25,055 (shared rows: 0%)
  - separates them from the shared rows: `displayName = ` on 25,055 of 25,055 (shared rows: 0%)
  - separates them from the shared rows: `contactType = Business Account` on 25,055 of 25,055 (shared rows: 0%)
  - separates them from the shared rows: `defAddressId = null` on 25,041 of 25,055 (shared rows: 0%)
- 61 DataMart-only rows are tombstones.

**Measured 2026-09-30:** The extra rows are 'Business Account' contacts (the company contact every customer, supplier and lead has). ERP `/v1/contact` lists person contacts only; `active=true` and `active=false` together equal the default list, and `GET /v1/contact/{id}` answers 404 for the hidden ids. They carry data (full name on 25,043, phone on 12,320, email on 6,496). DataMart is the only source.
