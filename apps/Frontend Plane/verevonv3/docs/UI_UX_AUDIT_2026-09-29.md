# UI/UX audit — /chat, /support, /insights, /knowledge

Date: 2026-09-29
Scope: the four routes and the shell they share (icon rail, navbar, secondary
sidebar, buttons, tokens, motion). Source read: `src/features/{chat,support,
inbox,tickets,insights,knowledge,core}`, `src/shared/ui`, `src/app/App.tsx`,
`src/styles/global.css` (48,291 lines) and the two `chat-appearance-*.css`
files that ship shell styles.
Method: a live walk of each page in the in-app browser at
http://localhost:5173 (screenshots, computed styles, an interactive-element
size sweep, `document.getAnimations()`, in-app navigation timing, a 15 s
focus-retention test on the Support list, and an injected `.dark` class to
measure dark-mode token coverage), followed by five parallel read-only source
audits (one per page, one for the shell) run against the checklists of the
apple-design, make-interfaces-feel-better, interface-design,
interaction-design, interactive-hit-areas, emil-design-eng,
solidjs-vite-typescript, improve-animations, find-animation-opportunities,
accessible-animation and mastering-animate-presence skills.
Review mode: full. Stack: SolidJS 2 rc, Vite 8, `@solidjs/router` 2, no motion
library (all motion is CSS). Styling: one global stylesheet with `verevon-*` /
`core-*` classes; every fix below is written in that system.
Verification state: no source file was modified. Commit `83927eda`; the
working tree has unrelated uncommitted edits in `global.css` near lines
7102–7148 and 24261, so line numbers may drift by a few lines.
Confidence marks: ✔ = re-read at the cited line by the author and/or observed
live; ◦ = reported by the source-audit pass, not independently re-read.

Measurement note: `.core-product-shell` applies `zoom: 0.9` at ≥768px
(`global.css:552–562`), so on-screen sizes are 90% of CSS sizes (a 36px
control renders at 32.4px). Sizes below are CSS px unless marked "on screen".

## 1. Verdict in one paragraph

The four pages share a sound visual language (one neutral surface, one coral
accent, quiet borders, dense 32–36px controls), and the per-page work is
mostly a matter of finishing details. But five defects are outright broken
rather than unpolished, and four of them are one- or two-line fixes: the
active filter chips on Insights are black-on-black because
`--verevon-primary-foreground` was never defined; the Knowledge folder cards
are crushed and their stats clipped ("183 Inde") by a second rule block for the
same selector; the Support conversation list is rebuilt from scratch every 15 s
so keyboard focus and text selection are lost (verified live); the only focus
ring in the app is an 18% black outline that nobody can see; and the brand
typeface never reaches the shell because `--font-sans` begins with an undefined
variable. Underneath those sit three systemic gaps: dark mode is 692 per-
selector patches with no token swap (Insights has zero dark rules), there is
no motion vocabulary (no easing or duration tokens, two `transition: all`
rules, a 440 ms animation on CTRL+K), and hit areas rely on a
`--verevon-touch-target` token that is used three times. Verdict: **Block**
until the HIGH rows are fixed; most of them are under an hour each.

### Live verification result (signed in, later on 2026-09-29)

Every plan was checked on the running app; the per-plan results and the exact
measurements are in `plans/README.md`. Two findings changed the
implementation:

- The focus ring from plan 002 drew at 1 device pixel because the shell runs at
  `zoom: 0.9` and Chrome floors outline widths; a zoom-compensated
  `--focus-ring-width` token fixed it.
- The Knowledge tree from plan 004 had an inverted hierarchy (a child's text
  started left of its parent's), caused by hiding icons at depth 2 and deeper
  and by reserving the chevron column only when a sibling had children. Icons
  now stay at every depth and every child row reserves the column. Deep labels
  are narrower as a result (86 to 98px) and lean on the tooltip.

One methodological note for anyone repeating this: on the dev server, hot
updates triggered by other sessions' edits remount components, so remount
tests (focus surviving a refresh, Copy not destroying the message) only mean
something on a static production build.
