# Product marketing context

Last updated: 3 November 2026. **Working draft for distribution planning, not approved public copy or verified product capability.**

## Product overview

Verevon is a governed work environment where people and AI coordinate work across company systems, knowledge and working rules. Existing systems keep their ownership; chat is one entry point. Proposed initial commercial package: prepare service jobs from incoming requests and relevant company evidence. Do not sell a live capability solely because a web mock depicts it.

## Audience and jobs

Founder-selected starting market: **Norwegian industrial and service companies**. Recommended first discovery segment: technical service and maintenance suppliers, initially 20–200 employees, subject to interviews. Adjacent comparison: industrial equipment/component suppliers.

Jobs to validate: find the relevant contract/order and prior work; identify missing information; prepare responsibility, deadline and customer reply; coordinate authorized follow-up. One recurring workflow, one team and one common systems combination first.

## Buying roles

| Role | Priority / evidence needed |
| --- | --- |
| Coordinator / user | Less searching, copying and chasing; useful prepared cases with manageable review |
| Service or operations manager / champion | Response time, backlog, predictable handoffs and team capacity |
| Owner, managing director or finance / economic buyer | Verified value, bounded cost and a clear continuation decision |
| IT owner or external adviser / technical influencer | Accurate data access, working integrations, authority and support responsibility |

## Problem, alternatives and differentiation

Problem hypothesis: recurring coordination is spread across inbox, files and business systems. Cost and frequency are not yet verified. Alternatives include the current coordinator process, ERP/service workflow, Microsoft tools already licensed, generic AI tools and integration consulting. Do not claim these cannot perform the task; benchmark the real alternative in each pilot.

Proposed differentiation: a repeatable industry workflow, source-visible preparation, clear authority, dependable implementation and named customer help. Those are commercial hypotheses until the connected product and measured results support them.

## Commercial hypotheses

NOK excluding VAT: four-week scoped pilot 15,000; initial ongoing package test 7,500/month with a defined usage cap; standard setup 10,000, separately costed. Not approved prices. See [unit economics](../plans/distribution/2026-11-03/04-unit-economics.md) for full cost and pilot-credit treatment.

## Objections and fit

- «Vi har allerede Microsoft/ERP.» Compare the actual end-to-end task and implementation cost. Integrate only where there is justified additional value.
- «Vi kan ikke gi tilgang til alt.» Scope evidence and authority narrowly; use authorized samples/exports for discovery. Do not promise controls that are unverified.
- «Dette blir enda et IT-prosjekt.» Offer one bounded workflow, a named owner, a baseline, cost cap and stop/continue decision.

Poor fits: generic AI experimentation without an operational owner, low task volume/value, compulsory ERP replacement, unbounded custom work, or high-stakes autonomous actions outside verified product authority.

## Switching dynamics

Push hypothesis: missed handoffs, repetitive investigation and coordinator overload. Pull hypothesis: prepared jobs and a visible next step. Habit: email, spreadsheets and existing ERP. Anxiety: wrong output, unauthorized action, integration burden and uncertain subscription value. Validate all through recent examples rather than asking abstractly whether people want AI.

## Voice and customer language

Calm, practical, precise and operator-oriented Norwegian. Use concrete tasks and real evidence. Avoid model superiority, vague transformation language, guaranteed savings, invented customer quotes and calling customers “boring” in public copy. No verbatim customer research is available yet.

Proposed message: «Fra servicehenvendelse til klargjort jobb.» Proposed invitation: «Finn en oppgave vi kan teste sammen.» These are drafts, not testimonials.

## Proof and retention

No verified commercial metrics, authorized customer logos or testimonials are established by this task. Product launch promises are requirements, not implementation evidence. Retain customers through useful routines, dependable service and sustained measurable value, with usable exports and clear exits.

## Goals and distribution

First learning objective: choose a repeatable workflow, complete two scoped paid pilots if readiness and buyers permit, and validate a production subscription decision. Primary channel: founder-led discovery and delivery. Secondary: trusted advisers and customer introductions. Collaboration acquisition and marketplace listings are later, gated hypotheses.
