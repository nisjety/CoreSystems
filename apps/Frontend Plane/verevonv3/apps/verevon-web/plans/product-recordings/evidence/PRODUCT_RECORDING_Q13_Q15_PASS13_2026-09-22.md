## Changes

- **Shorter reporting after private repair:** a complete source review that rejects some passages now retains its valid passage assessments privately for that artifact check. Only an unchanged passage with matching sources, request/requirements, checker, run/org, model/connection and privacy binding is eligible. A changed paragraph invalidates its entire contiguous Markdown section; child sections are also bound to ancestor headings. Passages with common English/Norwegian reference words always require a fresh full assessment. This is conservative eligibility filtering, not a factual verdict. The next reviewer receives the entire current document and all sources and must explicitly re-evaluate and confirm every reused index, including conflicts and dependencies involving changed text. Missing, duplicate, foreign or ineligible confirmations are rejected. No claim passes automatically because its text matches. These checks reduce known reuse risks without proving that every semantic dependency has been found.
- **Exact coverage and evidence:** receipt version `attachment-source-review-v8` binds the accepted complete content and original exact source spans, recording `reconfirmedSegments`. A partial report cannot publish a receipt. Private prior assessments are neither persisted nor shared across runs; cancellation and failure still prevent publication. When fewer than 12 passages need full returned assessments, re-review uses one full-context call; initial long-document reviews retain the two-call bound.
- **Scope and source inventory:** shared authoring/repair/review rules distinguish role-domain contingencies from undocumented possible product benefits. Role names establish ordinary work domains without inventing unrelated responsibilities. Explicit absence of documented evidence receives a source-span annotation, not an automatic verdict. The reviewer receives the actual attachment inventory so a report's source-basis note does not require an attachment to describe the report's writing. That inventory never proves external verification, business actions or unavailable source contents.
- **No extra generation after checked artifact completion:** once durable completion succeeds, the first thread title comes from the accepted artifact's sanitized title. Optional title and suggestion inference no longer hold up the terminal completion of a deterministic artifact receipt. Those two calls previously ran after the answer and could add up to 4 + 4 seconds; they did not compete with drafting. Ordinary chat retains its existing auxiliary generation and route/privacy handling.
- **Independent sales acceptance:** the gross-profit parser now binds each monetary change to its sentence and metric, and recognizes explicit increases/decreases outside parentheses. Previously it could read past a profit-decrease sentence and treat a later revenue increase as that profit change. Explicit wrong signs and amounts still fail. Maintained browser specs require the new checker version. No frontend runtime code or source fixture changed.
