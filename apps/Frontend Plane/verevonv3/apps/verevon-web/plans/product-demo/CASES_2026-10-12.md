## Phone hazard (the canvas ping-pong)

On a phone-width demo (390 px) the Work canvas opens as a full sheet over the chat, so the
approval gate is hidden. A first fix, a MutationObserver that clicks the canvas trigger closed,
appeared to start a ping-pong with the product's own auto-summon (the page stopped
responding), and was REMOVED. The replacement must be loop-proof by construction: either
CSS only (hide or collapse the sheet under a narrow media query inside the demo build, keeping
the trigger usable), or a script with a hard budget (at most 2 folds per message the visitor
sends, counted from that send, never from DOM mutations). It must be verified in a real browser
at 390×844 with every wait bounded, on all three cases, reaching the approval gate and
Godkjenn, and the check must fail (not hang) if the page stops responding.
