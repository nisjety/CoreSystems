# Verevon distribution plan

Prepared 3 November 2026. **Internal working drafts. Nothing in this package is published, sent, deployed or an approved customer commitment.**

## The decision

Start with **Norwegian industrial and service companies**, as selected by the founder. Test **technical service and maintenance suppliers with 20–200 employees** as the first segment. The employee range and narrower segment are recommendations to validate, not findings from customer research.

Sell one recurring operational result: **an incoming service request becomes a prepared job, with sources, missing information, responsibility, deadline and a customer reply ready for review.** Build a repeatable industry package on Verevon's shared platform.

Verevon can remain a broad, governed work environment. Its first sales offer must be specific enough that an operations manager understands what they are buying and can measure whether it works.

## Operating decisions

- Effort allocation hypothesis: 60% customer discovery/direct sales, 25% delivery and reusable onboarding, 15% adviser/referral learning. Do not run five channels simultaneously.
- First discovery sample: ten service/maintenance businesses and five adjacent industrial suppliers. Choose one repeatable workflow and one common systems combination from the evidence.
- Initial delivery capacity assumption: two concurrent pilots; a third starts after one finishes. One founder owns commercial work. Name a technical delivery owner before a live pilot.
- Pricing, budget, partner incentives, customer promises and success thresholds below are **test proposals**. There is no known budget approval, verified revenue cohort or agreed pilot price in the reviewed materials.
- Existing local launch requirements remain binding: [launch-promises.md](../../launch-promises.md). A mock, screenshot or video does not establish a live integration or authorization capability.

## The next decision meeting

1. Confirm the founder's actual access to 15 suitable companies; permission to use a logo or relationship is not assumed.
2. Choose two potential workflows for discovery: service-request preparation and order/delivery exception follow-up.
3. Review the first ten interviews before committing to an integration or industry landing page.
4. Approve a price test and delivery budget only after measuring task volume, value and implementation effort.

The 90-day clock starts when execution is separately authorized and a delivery owner is available. Research and preparation here do not start customer outreach.
