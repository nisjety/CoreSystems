# Research and evidence register

Reviewed 3 November 2026. Primary sources below establish specific practices, not a causal proof of business success. Company disclosures describe their own model and risks. Historical sources are deliberately historical. Recommendations, pricing and experiment thresholds in this package are our hypotheses.
