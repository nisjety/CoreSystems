Change Detection Precision

Three sentences make up the body of this article.

The first paragraph asserts that whitespace-only changes do not constitute a real delta.

The second paragraph asserts that paragraph reordering without content change is not a real delta either.

Why this matters

Pages frequently re-render with cosmetic changes — extra whitespace from a build pipeline, reordered widgets, normalized punctuation. Treating these as content deltas wastes downstream work.

The text-fingerprint and paragraph-diff layers exist precisely to suppress these false positives.
