The second paragraph discusses technical details. Web scraping systems must balance extraction quality with performance. A good scraper extracts the main content while filtering out navigation, ads, and other boilerplate elements.

Key Findings

Our evaluation shows that readability-based extraction produces cleaner output than naive HTML-to-markdown conversion. The key metrics are:

  • Content precision: how much of the extracted text is actual content
  • Content recall: how much of the actual content is extracted
  • Boilerplate ratio: percentage of output that is navigation/ads/chrome

These metrics form the basis of the Quarry evaluation scoreboard.

Conclusion

Automated extraction quality measurement is essential for scraping system development. Without benchmarks, regressions go undetected until users report them.
