## Proposed ledger text (lead lands it; WS16.S0 DR-15.1, status `proposed`)

Title: **Quarry reaches the Model Plane through one allowlisted, org-bound, metered machine lane**
- Decision: `service:quarry-edge` may call `POST /v1/ai/chat` (and only the routes in `SERVICE_ROUTE_GRANTS`, golden-pinned in `api/contracts/model-gateway-service-routes.json`). Two registry audiences, `model-gateway` (`models:invoke`) and `inference-core` (`inference:invoke`, `models:invoke`), retention `tenant`. Quarry sends the run's ZDR flag explicitly; the gateway meters a non-ZDR call to cost-core under the org (`MODEL_GATEWAY_AI_CHAT_METERING`, default off until the first live extraction) and rate-limits a service in its own `<org>|svc:<service>` bucket (`MODEL_GATEWAY_SERVICE_RPM`).
- Not chosen: opening `/v1/invoke` to services (it writes page text to a durable session thread, hard-codes sampling, refuses `max_cost_usd` under ZDR); a gateway-held inference principal; flipping `model-invoke`, `model-stream`, `model-audio` to granted.
- Deliberately undecided: AI-format pricing (`USAGE_LLM_METRICS`), whether ZDR extraction is ledgered (default: no, nothing published), org `injection_defense` and required PII redaction on this lane.
- Status `proposed`. Trigger: WS16.S6 tier E2 passes. Optional entry: `gateway.proto` `ExtractStructuredResponse.extracted_json` is not validated by the gateway (comment-only, send as an entry).
