## Depth

1. **No `constraints` or `clamped[]` in the crawl answer.** The task text expected them "if present". They are not. The answer reports a clamp only through `limits.*.clamped_by` (`default` or `hard`). A default clamp is not repeated as a warning (`effective.warnings` is `[]`, the status document `warnings` is `[]`). A client that wants to know "my `max_pages:0` became 1000" must read `limits.max_pages.requested` and `clamped_by`. Not a FAIL.
2. **A replay is invisible on the edge wire.** The first and second answer of the keyed pair are both HTTP 200 with the same body shape (only `accepted_at` differs). The replay shows only in Control (200 instead of 201) and in the missing usage event. `HandoffAck.replayed` is `#[serde(skip)]` on purpose (`routes.rs`, near line 1330). A client cannot tell a replay from a first submit. Not a FAIL; worth knowing for the SPA and the gateway.
3. **The qv2e NATS has no `usage.>` stream.** With billing-core absent, `js.publish` gets no stream acknowledgement, so the publisher's task has nothing to confirm. A plain subscriber still saw every event. In the real stack billing-core owns the stream. This stack therefore proves what the edge publishes, not what billing-core stores or dedupes.
4. **Control-down failure is clean.** The 502 returns in about 4 s (the edge waits for the connect error). No job row, no usage event, and the same status for a keyed and an unkeyed request.
5. **First SC7 job counts `failed` 1.** Job `job_01M61QZVY04YPRBQYXX58C6TVE` finished with 9 pages completed and 1 failed (not looked into; outside this leg). The job state is `completed`, `run_completed`.
