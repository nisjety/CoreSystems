# Depth bullets, unit W3-B3-03 (E3.6-10, document parsing and PII fixture corpus)

Written during the build (no depth runs). Format: severity, kind, what | where | why deferred.

- [severity high kind evidence] The corpus is synthetic. No reader, classifier threshold (MIN_TEXT_CHARS = 20) or phone and identity-number candidate is measured on a real Norwegian public-sector document, so there are no precision or recall numbers | apps/Ingestion Plane/Quarry-v2/crates/quarry-transform/tests/fixtures/documents/ | needs real samples the owner approves; a GDPR-safe source for them is a decision.
- [severity high kind test] Nothing runs pii::scan over the corpus against its recorded truth. The loader checks that the values are in the text and that they pass the validators, not that the scanner finds them or leaves the near misses alone | apps/Ingestion Plane/Quarry-v2/crates/quarry-transform/tests/document_corpus.rs | that test is the measuring step, and the fail-closed decision (E3.6-4) decides what a miss means; the estimate does not name it.
- [severity medium kind test] The corpus tests have never run. The expected page kinds (pages) are the author's application of the classifier rule, and the footer-over-scan case (no-06) depends on how lopdf reads the vector footer | apps/Ingestion Plane/Quarry-v2/crates/quarry-transform/tests/document_corpus.rs (corpus_pdf_pages_classify_as_recorded) | end-of-wave run; correct the manifest by regenerating, not by hand.
- [severity medium kind risk] Scanned pages are clean rasters drawn with Pillow's default font. Real scans (skew, noise, JPEG artefacts, colour, two-column layout) are not represented, so OCR results on this set will flatter any OCR route | no-04, no-05 page 2, no-06 in corpus.json | the OCR route is a later slice; needs real scans.
- [severity medium kind evidence] No PDF with CID or ToUnicode fonts, no encrypted PDF, and no Word or scanner-produced file. The lossy Tj/TJ text risk the plan names for E3.6 is therefore untested | apps/Ingestion Plane/Quarry-v2/crates/quarry-transform/tests/fixtures/documents/no/ | needs real samples.
- [severity medium kind decision] Email local parts with non-ASCII letters (æøå) are not in the corpus, and pii.rs accepts ASCII local parts only, so a Norwegian name in an address is under-redacted | apps/Ingestion Plane/Quarry-v2/crates/quarry-transform/src/pii.rs (EMAIL_RE, is_email) | decision with the owner on the public-sector corpus.
- [severity medium kind decision] Organisation numbers (974 760 673) and the KID are outside the ground truth on purpose. The rule (an organisation number is not personal data, a personal mobile is) is the author's; the PII owner should confirm it | no-03, no-07 in corpus.json | decision.
- [severity medium kind risk] The national phone candidate has no context check, and the corpus has no negative of its shape (a product or reference number grouped 2-2-2-2 or 3-2-3) apart from the order numbers, which have a different grouping | apps/Ingestion Plane/Quarry-v2/crates/quarry-transform/src/pii.rs (PHONE_NATIONAL_RE); corpus no-07 | add negatives once real samples show the shape.
- [severity medium kind hardening] The raster bytes and the scan files' sha256 depend on Pillow's default font, so a Pillow upgrade changes corpus.json's hashes and the loader test fails until the set is regenerated | apps/Ingestion Plane/Quarry-v2/crates/quarry-transform/tests/fixtures/documents/generate.py (render_scan) | regeneration recipe is in docs/DOCUMENT_CORPUS.md; pin Pillow in a dev requirements file if the set changes often.
- [severity low kind test] The DOCX fixtures are minimal (document.xml only, no styles, numbering, headers, footers or settings). Word-saved files have more parts, so the DOCX budgets and extraction are untested on real Word output | no-08, no-09 | add a Word-saved sample when one is approved.
- [severity low kind test] The set has no English or mixed-language document. English PII cases live only in tests/fixtures/pii/golden.json | apps/Ingestion Plane/Quarry-v2/crates/quarry-transform/tests/fixtures/documents/ | English set is a later slice.
- [severity low kind doc] The generator needs Pillow and Python 3 on the machine that regenerates the set. It is not wired into CI, and nothing checks that generate.py and corpus.json agree other than the loader's sha256 test | apps/Ingestion Plane/Quarry-v2/crates/quarry-transform/tests/fixtures/documents/generate.py | CI wiring belongs with the eval job (W3-B1-01 lane), not this unit.
- [severity low kind hardening] Scanned pages are one grey plane at 1000 x 1414. Colour scans, multi-page TIFF-like inputs and very large rasters (the image budgets in pdf.rs) are not in the set | apps/Ingestion Plane/Quarry-v2/crates/quarry-transform/tests/fixtures/documents/no/no-04-skannet-innkalling.pdf | add with the OCR route.
