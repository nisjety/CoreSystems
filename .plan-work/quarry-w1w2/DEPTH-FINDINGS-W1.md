How the register is built. The harvest held several hundred raw bullets; many repeat one defect from two or three notes. Rows are de-duplicated and some medium and high rows hold several closely related raw items (they are written out in the "what" cell, so none is dropped). Low items are grouped by theme, one row per theme, because none of them changes a decision. Severity is the note's own, raised only where several notes agree. IDs are D1-001 and up, in the order shown (severity, then kind).

Counts: 87 rows: 16 high, 64 medium, 7 low (the low rows are themes of many small items).

Kinds: bug (defect in code that exists), risk (a hazard no test or decision covers), evidence (a run on a stack that the plan requires and nobody did), test (a missing or weak test), decision (owner or ADR), other-plane (Model Plane, Control Plane or sibling session), outside-branch (GitHub, counsel, merge, shared tree), hardening, doc. Effort: S = under a day, M = one to three days, L = more than three days or spread over many files.
