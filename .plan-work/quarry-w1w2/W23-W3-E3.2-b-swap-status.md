## Assumed decisions (owner said stop asking)

1. Swap behind a build flag rather than replacing the old cards outright (new behaviour ships dark).
2. The parents keep owning the decide call: the card's `decide` prop forwards to `onDecideBrowserApproval` / the deck's `onDecide` and resolves at once, so no second `decideApproval` call and no change to the parents' state. Consequence: the card's own "Sending..." and retry-on-failure states do not show for these mounts (the parent's error handling applies), and in BrowserChrome the parent's in-flight key no longer disables the card's buttons (depth row).
3. The chat deck keeps its own card for approvals with a password step-up or a standing scope; the shared card has neither control.
4. Decided browser gates keep the old card (it is the decided marker).
5. `allowRedirect` stays false; the MP ask asks execution-core to read redirect text from a rejected gate (`gate_risky_action`) with the same 1000-character cap.
