## Flag and default

`QUARRY_EDGE__DOWNLOADS`, default **off**. Off: contexts keep the earlier allowAndName quarantine and `downloads_to_artifacts` stays false, so real runs keep refusing `download_ref` at the capability gate. On: default-deny contexts, one download per verified `download_ref`, capability advertised, and the E3.4-4 edge admission policy on with it.

## Assumed decisions

1. One switch for the whole path: the driver reuses `QUARRY_EDGE__DOWNLOADS` (E3.4-4's flag) rather than a new browser flag, so admission and the driver go on together (D-E346-4).
2. Flag on advertises `downloads_to_artifacts` (the proof is written, not run; the flag stays off until it is run).
3. Unsolicited = any download not started by the verified target's click in its frame; denied by context behaviour, and cancelled while a `download_ref` holds the context open.
4. Single use at the driver = one open download per session at a time, the context closed again after it, and the quarantine copy deleted after reading. The grant's single use stays the existing sensitive-approval spend.
5. A failed deny restore leaves the earlier quarantine behaviour, logged, not a disposed context (D-E346-2).
6. The Chromium proof uses `set_content` + `data:` markup mirroring the test-site fixture, because loopback navigation is refused by the egress guard (D-E346-5).
