## Gaps vs the mission

1. No slice makes the production overlay boot a browser-capable quarry-edge: artifact backend, `DURABLE_EVENT_HISTORY` (K2-01) and the writable Chromium root (WS3.A) are three separate defects owned by three slices or none; the mission rule "a deployment claim counts only after a verified end-to-end flow on rebuilt images" cannot be met for the production overlay until a single owned slice (`WS1.A0`) closes all three.
2. New user-visible refusals in WS6 (402 `org_allowance`, 429 `org_backlog_full`) and WS5 (415 `UNSUPPORTED_MEDIA` on crawl pages) have no gateway normalisation for the crawl route; the shared browser and Firecrawl-class crawl experience would show raw Quarry envelopes (K2-07).
3. Generated and vendored contract copies have no owner: `python/mp-events-py/.../browser_pb2.py`, the TypeScript gen tree under `Frontend Plane/verevon`, and the Quarry vendored `browser.proto` (no sync tool, no parity test) (K2-04).
4. Route-completeness gates (WS4.G matrix, WS5.H OpenAPI coverage) are not robust to the WS2.B module split or to test-only routes, so the "ZDR matrix covers every route" and "OpenAPI covers every route" guarantees would fail or silently shrink (K2-05).
5. The gateway `AppState` growth across WS1.B, WS8.D, WS8.I is not planned as one mechanical change; three slices will each break six test literals (K2-03).
6. The mission requires the human and the AI to share one governed browser; WS8.F relies on `execution-core` minting `aud=quarry` service tokens with `browser:execute` (already true) but nothing in WS8 states that `human_browse` ZDR sessions (X-2, memory-only grant on one broker replica) survive a broker restart with an actively attached agent; the evidence list never restarts the broker mid-attach.
