### F-t2-003 · Close the gateway search-cache ZDR and tenant hole, then publish a per-feature ZDR capability matrix
| Tier | Verevon today | Effort | Plane | Wave | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Priority | Partial | M | Frontend Plane gateway (phase 1); Ingestion Plane (matrix, tombstone) | W1 | High |

**What to build.** Phase 1 (S): promote the gateway's private CacheAdmission to a shared module, call it in search_web before lookup and before store, add the authorized org id to the cache key, and key the scrape-preview cache by org and request variant (formats, headers, actions). Phase 2 (M): generate or test a per-feature ZDR capability matrix from route attributes in quarry-core with explicit refusal codes, and add a metadata-only ZDR run tombstone with a retention janitor after a contract review.

**Why it matters.** Two architecture rules are broken at once. The gateway `/search` cache has no org in its key, so an answer built from one org's private corpus can be served to another org, and it stores with no ZDR check. This is source-verified cross-tenant exposure, and the gateway is the one boundary that must hold neither. Phase 1 is a small gateway-only change plus a test.

**What the references do.** Firecrawl has the only explicit cache-admission rule, 1 of 4 references: `shouldCache` is an inline boolean inside `sendDocumentToIndex`. It refuses to cache when the request carries custom headers, actions or a profile, when the result came from a low-fidelity engine, from Exchange or from the index itself, and for page-aware or marker PDF parsers [SRC]. Firecrawl also has the only per-feature ZDR list, 1 of 4 references: team modes are disabled, allowed or forced; agent, extract and scrape-status refuse under forced ZDR, and change tracking degrades with a warning [SRC]. Deletion is a per-request ZDR cleanup queue plus access-row expiry (24 h default, `crawlTtlHours` for crawls), with URL and options redacted in the request row while outcome, timing and cost remain [SRC]. Plan-based retention comes from Apify and is documentation only [DOC]. Aside shows local session storage and a 15-minute purge of terminal ephemeral sessions, not a retention policy [SRC]. Caveat: the ZDR team modes are enterprise flags, and not every ZDR sink's retention was traced.

**Verevon today.** search_web keys on query, limit, include_answer and filters only (apps/Frontend Plane/verevonv3/apps/gateway/src/domains/search.rs:209-226), stores with no ZDR check (search.rs:285) and keeps entries 4 h fresh and 24 h stale (cache.rs:20-25). The Quarry `/v1/search` it fronts uses org-scoped private-corpus providers (quarry-edge/src/search_routes.rs:503-517). Quarry itself is correct: its SearchCache is org-keyed and skips reads and writes under ZDR (search_routes.rs:762-780). A ZDR-aware CacheAdmission exists privately for the scrape preview (knowledge/quarry.rs:213-280), though its key (line 104) is target-only. Reuse `crate::zdr::{request_zdr, org_zdr_enabled}` (fails closed), `authorized_org_id`, the existing refusal codes (quarry-edge/src/routes.rs:563-588) and the quarry-control janitor loop.

**How to build it in Verevon.** The gateway owns phase 1; Quarry owns the matrix and tombstone. Fail closed on a ZDR lookup failure, as zdr.rs does. The tombstone must be metadata only. Do not weaken Verevon's stricter refusals when aligning with Firecrawl. ZDR semantics for caches, monitors and schedules need the ADR shared with [F-t2-004](#F-t2-004), [F-t2-016](#F-t2-016) and [F-t2-030](#F-t2-030). Risks: lower cross-org hit rate (acceptable) and matrix drift (generate or test it).

**Quick win.** The highest-value small fix in the audit: a small gateway-only change, existing helpers, one test that a ZDR org's search leaves no cache entry.
