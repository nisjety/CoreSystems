### F-t2-011 · Let users browse freely: a human-session policy plus an omnibox that routes free text to Verevon search
| Tier | Verevon today | Effort | Plane | Wave | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Priority | Partial | L | Ingestion Plane (Quarry) with Model Plane broker and Frontend Plane omnibox | W2 | High |

**What to build.** Two deliverables. (a) Frontend slice (S to M): URL-like omnibox input navigates, free text opens Verevon search through the existing gateway search route or Ask into chat, a suggestion dropdown is fed by the gateway suggest route plus local history, and hard reload with error-page retry is added. (b) Backend slice (L): a human-browsing grant kind in the Model Plane BrowserBroker that Quarry validates instead of exact domain-list equality, with renewal for idle sessions, and Quarry human-session semantics that exempt human steps from `max_steps` and use an idle timeout instead of a 120 s lease. Egress and SSRF (server-side request forgery) policy stay unchanged.

**Why it matters.** A browser locked to its first host for 120 seconds is not a browser, and today free text in the address bar becomes a google.com URL that the domain lock rejects. This is the largest day-one usability gap for people.

**What the references do.** Codex's in-app browser is the model. It ships back, forward, reload and hard reload (ignore cache) as commands, its preload captures mouse buttons 3 and 4, and reload from an error page retries the failed URL [SRC]. Its omnibox is a token-based suggestion service: `start(query)` streams matches with contents, description, destination URL and accept and delete tokens; `accept(token)` navigates and records; `deleteMatch` removes a history entry [SRC]. Navigation controls are recorded in 1 of 4 references (Codex) and inferred for Aside as a Chromium browser [INF]; omnibox suggestions are in 2 of 4 (Codex, Aside). Aside wraps the upstream Chromium omnibox in a private extension API with an AI mode that attaches tabs [SRC]. Free cross-site human browsing in Codex is inferred from the absence of a URL allowlist for human navigation [INF]. Caveats: the Codex browser pane and browser-use are remote-config gated and default to false; its autocomplete uses an API stock Electron lacks, so a patched Chromium omnibox and its suggestion sources are unverified [INF].

**Verevon today.** Back, forward, soft reload, new tab and a human-takeover control mode ship (apps/Frontend Plane/verevonv3/src/features/dashboard/home/BrowserChrome.tsx:1143-1215; apps/Frontend Plane/verevonv3/apps/gateway/src/domains/browser.rs:255-300, 690-790), but every user session is created with `allowed_domains` = first host, `max_steps` 12 and `max_runtime_s` 120 (browser.rs:483-490), and Quarry applies the same budget and domain list to human steps (apps/Ingestion Plane/Quarry-v2/crates/quarry-edge/src/agent_routes.rs:446-451, 2258-2300). In governed mode the broker list must be non-empty, at most 32 hosts and equal on every step (quarry-browser/src/lib.rs:55-77; agent_routes.rs:314-339), and grants expire after 15 minutes (apps/Model Plane/go/services/browser-broker/internal/server/server.go:22). The omnibox turns free text into a google.com URL (browser-omnibox.ts:14-15) with no suggestions. Reuse `BrowserControlMode` and timeline initiator tagging, the gateway search/web, suggest and suggestions routes (search.rs:36-70, 100-140), the CrossDomainNavigation approval gate, the egress private-IP blocks and the `PersistentSessionRegistry` idle-reaper pattern (persistent_session.rs:55-110).

**How to build it in Verevon.** Host authority is broker-only in governed mode, so the policy is a Model Plane BrowserBroker feature validated by Quarry; neither the gateway nor Quarry may widen the list alone, and the item depends on [F-t2-001](#F-t2-001) and shares idle and absolute TTL work with [F-t2-035](#F-t2-035). Agent-initiated cross-domain navigation keeps its HITL gate, and human-versus-agent origin comes from Quarry's control mode, never a client flag. ZDR: no local or server history or suggestions. The gateway search cache needs org and user keys and ZDR gating before it feeds suggestions ([F-t2-003](#F-t2-003)). Space access must not widen a user's host policy. Risks: SSRF and egress abuse, unbounded host growth, cost of long-lived Chromium leases without idle reaping.
