### F-t2-009 · Route PDFs and Office files to the right parser in /v1/scrape (Content-Type plus magic bytes)
| Tier | Verevon today | Effort | Plane | Wave | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Priority | Partial | S | Ingestion Plane (quarry-runtime pipeline, quarry-transform) | W1 | High |

**What to build.** After a driver returns a body, decide the content kind from the Content-Type header and then from the first bytes (`%PDF-`, a zip local-file header for Office Open XML). If the kind is PDF or DOCX, hand the already-fetched bytes to the matching quarry-transform parser instead of running charset decoding and readability over binary data, and record the detected kind in output metadata. Legacy OLE `.doc`, images, archives and media get a typed `unsupported_media` skip reason.

**Why it matters.** PDFs and Office files linked from public sites are a common knowledge-base input. Today a PDF URL goes through readability and produces markdown that users see is wrong at once, even though a PDF text extractor already ships in the answer path.

**What the references do.** Firecrawl is the only reference that routes files to parsers: 1 of 4 references, and table-stakes for a Firecrawl-class scraper [SRC]. Its `specialtyScrapeCheck` checks a cloud-storage PDF reference first, then the Content-Type header, then magic bytes from a signature table (`%PDF-`, `PK 03 04` for OOXML/ODF, OLE2 for legacy Office); the sniff window is derived from the table, and the zip check excludes genuine ZIP media types and does not look for `[Content_Types].xml` [SRC]. A non-HTML kind raises a typed handoff that carries the downloaded bytes, so the parser never re-downloads [SRC]. Archives, audio, video and executables are rejected explicitly; image routing needs a deployment switch plus a per-request parsers option, otherwise images are rejected [SRC]. Apify's Website Content Crawler selects files by content type but only downloads them and does not extract text [DOC]. Caveat: Firecrawl's PDF quality depends on the external FirePDF service in the hosted product [SRC].

**Verevon today.** `PageRunner` reads Content-Type only for the `PageFetched` event and charset decode, then runs readability over every body (apps/Ingestion Plane/Quarry-v2/crates/quarry-runtime/src/pipeline.rs:441-506). `is_pdf` (header or `%PDF-` in the first 1 KiB) lives in quarry-runtime/src/answer.rs:679-753, and answer.rs:839 is the only caller of `pdf::extract_text` (25 MB cap, quarry-transform/src/pdf.rs:40, 202-213). `docx::extract_text` exists with the same cap but has no non-test caller (quarry-transform/src/docx.rs:1-40). `images.rs` holds only HTML lazy-image and srcset helpers (images.rs:1-40), so there is no image parser to route to. Reuse `is_pdf`, the two parsers with their caps, `charset::decode`, the `PageFetched` content_type metadata and `zdr::guard`.

**How to build it in Verevon.** Ingestion Plane only. Add the sniff after the driver fetch in pipeline.rs, reuse the existing PDF sniffer, add the zip-header check confirmed by a `[Content_Types].xml` entry so plain zip archives are not sent to the DOCX parser, and route PDF and DOCX only. Keep the byte caps (16 MB pipeline body, 25 MB per parser), parser timeouts and zip decompression-bomb limits. Under Zero-Data-Retention (ZDR) extracted text stays inline with no artifact write. Record the kind in metadata because changed output for non-HTML bodies changes the fingerprints that change monitors compare. Image bodies need OCR or a vision call, a separate M-sized item; `services/quarry-vision-sidecar` is the likely home. Risk: large or malicious files, so enforce caps and sandbox parsers.

**Quick win.** The sniffer and both parsers exist; the work is wiring plus a typed skip reason.
