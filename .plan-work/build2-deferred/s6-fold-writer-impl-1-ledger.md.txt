**2026-10-18, S6 writer rules (WS03).** The Do loop's fold writer is built behind EXECUTION_LOOP_CHECKPOINT_ENABLED (default false; the strict parser accepts `true` only, case-insensitive, and is shared by every new durable-loop switch). It needs EXECUTION_EFFECT_LEDGER_ENABLED (without the ledger it logs once per run and writes nothing) and session-core's SESSION_CORE_LOOP_CHECKPOINT_ENABLED (without it the first save is refused `loop_checkpoint_disabled` and the writer turns itself off, which is switch-off behaviour). Roll out session-core first, execution-core second; roll back in reverse.
- Three save points, from the existing run_rounds locals (no LoopState refactor): `round` at the end of a round that ran tools (best effort: a failed save is skipped), `pre_effect` before a round dispatches any effectful call (fail closed: a failed save holds that round's effectful calls with a tool-only 'try again' text; the read calls of the batch still run and the next round retries), and `pause` inside pause_for_approval after the descriptor and BEFORE CreateApproval.
- `pause_ref` = the approval's idempotency key `{run_id}:{step_id}`, known before session-core mints the approval id; S7 claims with the same key derived from the consumed approval's step id. If CreateApproval fails after the pause fold saved, the fold is closed with `approval_not_created`.
- A pause whose fold could not be saved keeps the approval's `loop` mode; `/continue` falls back to stage 1 at claim time (`not_claimable`). The `segment` mode and the gateway preflight change were dropped.
- Store failures fail closed where an effect depends on them; content refusals (too large over a 3 MiB budget after one tier-1 clear, credential-shaped) degrade to a tombstone (`extensions.not_resumable`, kind `round`, replaced by the next ordinary save). Stale and closed (including `loop_checkpoint_run_ended`) stop the writer for the rest of the run and hold effects; a disabled or non-persistent store turns it off. One probe at `expected + 1` follows a stale answer after an ambiguous (timeout) failure.
- Never saved for a zero-retention run, a waived run, a Space run, a delegated loop (depth > 0) or a stage-1 continuation segment. Fold content is scrubbed per field (scrub_fold) with credential-shaped fields withheld; call arguments are saved byte-exact or empty, and an empty-argument call must never be redispatched. `prompt_digest` is blake3 over the code-owned system prompt and the sorted tool definitions (mp.prompt.v1), not over the skills block.
