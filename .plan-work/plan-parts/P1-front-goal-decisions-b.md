
## 2. Decisions first

Nothing in this plan waits for a decision to start. Every decision below has a recommended default that lets dependent slices be written and tested, and each record names what proceeds without it and what it gates (merge, production enablement or a default flip). Decisions are grouped by the first thing they block. Ids: `ADR-1..10` are the audit's section 9.3 topics; `OQ-1..15` are its section 10.2 open questions (those that restate an ADR point to it); `X-n` are decisions the designs raised that the audit does not list. Slice ids are the catalogue's letter ids (`WS7.H` is design D7's S7).

### 2.1 Decision summary table

"Blocks" says what the decision holds up: **W1 merge** (a W1 slice cannot merge), **W1 enable** (the slice merges, but a production default or flag flip waits), **W1 evidence** (a spike, not a ruling), **W2 merge**, **W2 enable**, **Later** (W3 and beyond). Blocking decisions come first. Where the architecture memo's gate list is stricter than the sequencing memo's `GATED` flags (WS1.A, WS8.F, WS8.G, the WS5.B default, WS12.D, WS9.H, WS17.D), the stricter list is used.
