Where the reconciliation list in arch-A section 2 (R-n) or a decision record (X-n, ADR-n, OQ-n) overrides a design, the override is used and named in one clause. Wave 1 workstreams are WS1 to WS5; later workstreams are cut the same way by the other section 3 parts.
