### 2.1 How to read the register

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| SIB-CODE | The sibling has already built it on its local branch (commit or file named). Space adapts; it does not re-decide. |
| SIB-PLAN | The sibling plan or ledger text settles it, nothing is built. Space should adopt it unless it records an objection now. |
| JOINT | Needs one ruling covering Space and at least one sibling (also listed in 2.4). |
| SPACE | Only Space slices are affected. |

"Proceeds without it" means: written, tested and merged dark behind the slice's own flag, with a placeholder where the decision lands. Slice ids are the designs' own (WSnn-Sk). Items marked UNVERIFIED could not be confirmed in code.
