**Plane ownership / ZDR / Model-proposes-Quarry-executes check:**
- Control Plane owns plan and org numbers (`quarry.cap.*`, allowance policies, per-run ceiling); Quarry enforces and never stores or computes entitlements, reads billing-core over one read-only principal and has no database access; the estimator rates are budget heuristics, not prices (prices stay in billing-core and Lago). Model Plane mints the grant claim and enforces the combined cap in execution-core; cost-core is not written in v1.
- ZDR: accumulators are memory-only and no checkpoint is written for ZDR; usage metadata under ZDR is an allowlist with no URL, host, title, fingerprint or query; metrics labels are closed sets; crawl and batch stay refused under ZDR so no ZDR backlog row exists (a test pins it); drain writes no ZDR checkpoint; a ZDR crash loses an in-memory segment (under-billing accepted).
- Quarry rejects: budgets, caps and clamps only tighten (`min`), `initiated_by` and class hints are tighten-only, and the budget gate runs before the single-use approval is consumed.
