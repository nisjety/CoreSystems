**Risks and mitigations**
- Trigger on `messages` slows appends or deadlocks: it updates a row the appender already holds `FOR UPDATE`; benchmark with and without; concurrent-append DB test.
- Backfill load on a large table: 500-row batches with sleep, resumable, window refuses until done, BFF degrades to legacy.
- Bump-on-reply ordering surprises paging users: keyset on tail sequence never skips, client dedupes by `thread_id`; D2 lets the owner choose creation order (changes the key in S5 and S6 only).
- Summary leaks counts to a lower audience ceiling: fail-closed `activity_max_audience_revision` predicate, fresh decision per page, DB test.
- One scroll root regresses the cockpit on narrow screens: S1 behind `features.timeline_anchor`, Playwright at 390 and 1280 px; Solid 2 RC reactivity traps in the store: two-phase `createEffect` pattern as `SpacePage.tsx:145-190`, spike before S8.
- Proto tag and migration collisions with WS01, WS02, WS07, WS08, WS10, WS11 and (b): only numbers from 04-sequencing 4.6; first lander defines shared fields; S6 needs the `include_first` tag confirmed.
- Preview spoofing or SSRF, and Quarry preview mode is another session's work: BFF cache indirection, host from URL, "Preview captured when sent" label, `safeHref`, SSRF pre-check, no images, rate limit; S10 stays conditional (X23). Ledger or notice source down: named gap in `unavailable`, never a failed timeline.
