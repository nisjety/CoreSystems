
## 3. Design decisions

Tags: **ADR-GATED** needs the owner's ruling before the named work ships (the audit's ten topics in 9.3 do not cover the grant: nearest are topic 1 for where authority is minted and topic 4 for ZDR semantics, so the grant is recorded as a **new** decision, DR-15.1 below). **DECISION** is a smaller choice to confirm that does not block the build. Ownership background: master-ownership-matrix rows 61-62 give scrape formats to Quarry and make the Model Plane the "Owner" of AI `summary`/`json`/`query` generation, with Quarry doing "bridge/output validation"; row 88 and rule 16 make cost-core the ledger of record and forbid a second ledger in Quarry.

### DEC-1. Which Model Plane route Quarry calls (DECISION, part of DR-15.1)

Options: (A) extend the existing `POST /v1/ai/chat` with additive, bounded fields and richer response; (B) add a dedicated service-only `POST /v1/ai/structured` shaped like the unused `StructuredExtractRequest` (`Q/quarry-core/src/contracts.rs:941-953`: `max_tokens`, `max_cost_usd`, required `zdr`, `source_trace_required`); (C) open `/v1/invoke` to service principals; (D) call the `ExtractStructured` RPC; (E) forward the caller's own user bearer instead of a service token.

Recommendation: **A now, B only if the Model Plane owner wants a dedicated capability** (the rerank ruling of 2026-10-03 prefers a dedicated, default-deny, service-only route). Rationale: `ai_chat` is already allowlisted for services, already non-persisting and already carries `structured_output_schema` and `zdr` (section 2.2); the additions it needs are small and benefit users too (bounded `max_tokens`, `temperature`, cost and provider in the response). Quarry keeps the call behind one function (`ModelPlaneClient::chat_for_org`) so moving to B is a one-line route change. (C) is rejected: it writes page text into durable Session Core threads (`http_routes.rs:9124-9150`) and widens the service allowlist for a route built for chat turns. (D) is gRPC-only with no first-party caller, and it fetches the page itself: it is the interim route (DEC-14), not the product route. (E) needs the delegated-bearer exchange of D1 (F-t2-001) and cannot serve schedules, crawls or API-key callers.

### DEC-2. The grant: shape, retention and who decides (ADR-GATED, new decision DR-15.1; adjacent audit topics 1 and 4)

Options for the grant shape:
- **G1, two audiences, doctrine-aligned (recommended):** `quarry-edge` gains `model-gateway: [models:invoke]` and `inference-core: [inference:invoke, models:invoke]`, both retention `tenant`. It matches how `verify_delegated_service_inference_bearer` is written and the retrieval-engine precedent (an existing principal already holds `inference:invoke` on `inference-core`). Cost: quarry-edge's credential then could also address inference-core directly, bypassing the gateway's route table, rate limit and metering; mitigations are network policy (inference-core unpublished; verify in compose), the `tenant` org binding, and the directory org check at every mint.
- **G2, one audience plus a gateway-held inference workload principal:** smaller Quarry authority, new ambient authority in the gateway (it would hold an `inference-core` principal for any org), contrary to the delegated-identity doctrine ("the verified user bearer above is not an authority for this managed-run lease", `http_routes.rs:9160-9163`), and `tenant` retention needs the org per mint anyway. Not recommended.
- **G3, no grant:** Model-proposed route (DEC-14) plus BFF composition with the user's own token. Serves chat and agents, not the Quarry REST API, schedules or crawls.
- **G4, status quo:** extraction only under `CROSS_PLANE_AUTH_DEV_BYPASS`; refused in production by `main.rs:176`.

Retention posture of both new lanes: **`tenant`** (token ZDR follows the org's live posture, 60 s tokens, 503 and no token when org-core is unreadable), plus an explicit `zdr:true` in the request body whenever Quarry's effective ZDR is on (floor from the caller's token, raised by the body flag). `persistent` alone would make a ZDR org's call non-ZDR unless Quarry's body flag were perfect; `zdr` alone would make every extraction ZDR-only and refuse providers that cannot attest. Tenant-staleness window is disclosed by Auth Core (about 105 s, `interactive-retention-policy.ts` header); the body flag closes it in the safe direction because ZDR only ever tightens.

Row-level separation: add **two new manifest rows** (`model-extract`, `model-extract-inference`) and leave `model-invoke`, `model-stream` and `model-audio` at `granted:false`. The rows are what `model_plane_gate` reads, so autoprompt, intent, page titles, answer synthesis and audio stay dark even though the registry audience exists, and their calls would 403 at the route table anyway. This keeps the blast radius of the decision to "structured extraction".

**Decision record DR-15.1 (draft for `MPX/docs/decisions/ledger.md`, state `proposed`, in the form of the 2026-10-03 and 2026-10-06 entries):**

> **Quarry reaches the Model Plane through one allowlisted, org-bound, metered machine lane (`POST /v1/ai/chat`).** *The decision.* `service:quarry-edge` may call `POST /v1/ai/chat` for structured extraction. The lane is the default-deny service-route table of `authorize_principal_route` plus one row; the grant is two registry audiences (`model-gateway`, `inference-core`) with retention `tenant`; Quarry sends the run's ZDR flag explicitly; the gateway meters the call to cost-core under the org (non-ZDR) and rate-limits services in their own bucket. *Not chosen:* `/v1/invoke` for services (durable threads), a gateway-held inference principal (ambient authority), flipping `model-invoke`, `model-stream`, `model-audio` (they carry six other features). *The rows:* `service:quarry-edge POST /v1/ai/chat needs models:invoke`; registry `quarry-edge` audiences `model-gateway [models:invoke]`, `inference-core [inference:invoke, models:invoke]`; manifest rows `model-extract`, `model-extract-inference`. *What bounds it:* contract specs applying the real rules (section 4, slices 1 and 2), the golden service-route table, `extract:ai` scope on Quarry's own routes, per-request and per-org budgets. *Deliberately not decided:* pricing of AI formats; whether ZDR extraction is cost-ledgered (no Model Plane envelope exists for ZDR turns); org `injection_defense` and PII policy on this lane (F-t2-050). *Trigger to flip to `implemented`:* live proof in section 4, slice 6, tier E2.

### DEC-3. Where schema normalisation, validation and the one repair pass live (DECISION)

Options: (a) Quarry; (b) an inference-core option (`repair:true`); (c) model-gateway. Recommendation: **(a) Quarry.** Repair is "validate, then one more call that carries the validator's issues": it needs the schema validator, which only Quarry has (C7), and it must not leak page text into the repair prompt. inference-core is a provider router with no business rules; putting repair there would require a validator in the Model Plane and couple provider code to output contracts. Quarry's role is "bridge/output validation" per matrix row 62. Provider fallback stays in inference-core's chain; Quarry adds none. Deterministic local repair (fence strip, first balanced object, trailing commas, numeric-string coercion) runs before any model repair. The interim route (DEC-14) needs a validator in model-gateway: a small port (`schema_check.rs`) pinned to Quarry's by a shared corpus (slice 8), not a shared crate, because the two workspaces have no common crate.

### DEC-4. Schema normalisation rules (DECISION)

`ai_schema::normalise(schema) -> Result<NormalisedSchema, Vec<FieldError>>`: size at most 64 KiB, depth at most 10, at most 200 properties in total; root must be `type: object` (arrays and scalars are a field error telling the caller to wrap); local `$ref` to `#/$defs/*` or `#/definitions/*` is **inlined** (depth-bounded, cycles are `schema_recursive`); any other `$ref` (remote, relative file, `$dynamicRef`) is `schema_ref_not_supported`; `$schema`, `$id`, `$comment` are stripped from the wire copy. Unlike Firecrawl's `normalizeSchema` (all keys required, `additionalProperties:false`), **do not force `required`**: forcing it makes the model invent values for fields the page does not contain, which is the failure the extraction prompt forbids. `additionalProperties` is forwarded only if the caller set it. A `ComplexityClass` (Simple, Complex: any `anyOf`/`oneOf`, depth above 4, more than 40 properties) picks the routing alias. Output: the resolved schema (prompt, validation, wire), its sha256 (for metrics and a future cache key; the schema is never logged).

### DEC-5. Provider strictness: send the schema both ways and validate after every call (DECISION)

OpenAI receives a non-strict `json_schema` (`openai.rs:463-488`); Anthropic receives no schema parameter at all (`anthropic.rs:731-737`); other providers behave per inference-core. So Quarry **always embeds the schema text in the prompt and also sends `structured_output_schema`**, never relies on the parameter, and validates every reply (local repair, then at most one model repair, then report). Not adopted: `strict:true` (the Model Plane ledger defers it for tools; for non-strict schemas with optional fields it would reject valid schemas) and a Quarry-side provider fallback (inference-core's chain already does that).

### DEC-6. Structured harvest first (DECISION: default `context`, flip to `prefill` on an eval gate)

The harvest (`StructuredData`, plus `JsonLdSummary`) is not schema-shaped (section 2.1), so "the model fills only missing fields" is only true for the narrow set that can be matched without a model. Design, in `harvest_map.rs`: (1) **context**: always append a compact, bounded harvest block (figures with unit and period, JSON-LD scalars, first table headers) to the untrusted page section, because the SSB incident shows facts that exist only in hydration state; (2) **prefill** (flag-controlled): for each top-level property of the caller schema of primitive type, take a value only when a JSON-LD, microdata or hydration key at depth 1 equals the property name after case, underscore and camelCase folding, the value is type-compatible, and an `enum` (if any) contains it; objects and arrays are never prefilled. Prefilled fields are removed from the model's target schema (`properties` and `required` minus prefilled); if nothing remains, **no model call is made**; the merged object is validated against the full original schema; provenance `{field, source, rule:"exact_name"}` is returned in `ai.json.prefilled`. Why not default `prefill`: JSON-LD is publisher-asserted and can disagree with the visible page (stale price); the same trust level as page text, but a silent override of what the model would read is a precision risk. Gate: flip when the fixture corpus (slice 5) shows at least 99 percent exact-name precision and no regression against model-only on the same pages.

### DEC-7. Untrusted page text and prompt framing (DECISION)

Page text and harvest are untrusted. Quarry: puts all trusted instructions (system rules, caller prompt, schema) in the system message and the page in the user message inside a delimiter containing a per-request random nonce (`<<<PAGE 3f9a...>>> ... <<<END 3f9a...>>>`, not the fixed `===` of today's prompts, which page text can close); states the page is data; sends no tools (`ai_chat` has none) so there is nothing to hijack; caps output tokens; validates every output against a schema the caller wrote; makes highlights and question quotes **verbatim-verified** (DEC-8); and never executes or renders model output. This reduces, it does not remove, injection risk: a page can still bend a field value, and the response says so (`ai.<fmt>.untrusted_source: true`). Gap stated: the org's `injection_defense` and PII policies are applied on `invoke` only (`http_routes.rs:8946`, `moderation.rs`), not on `ai_chat`, so they do not cover this lane; PII redaction before the model hop is F-t2-050's, and the contract with D5 is that **AI formats read post-redaction markdown** (section 5). No new Quarry-side injection classifier (rejected: policy is org-owned in capability-core and Quarry does not read it).

### DEC-8. Highlights and question must be verbatim (DECISION)

Highlights: the model returns `{"highlights":[{"quote": "..."}]}` under a schema; Quarry accepts a quote only if it occurs in the model-visible markdown after whitespace-run collapsing and a fixed fold of non-breaking space and typographic quotes, with at least 8 characters, and returns the **source slice** with `start` and `end` character offsets into the returned `markdown`, de-duplicated, ordered, non-overlapping. Dropped quotes are counted in `ai.highlights.dropped`; an empty verified set is a normal, honest result with warning `highlights_unverified`, never filled in from model text. Question: schema `{answer, supporting_quote, found}`; `grounded` is true only if the quote verifies; `found:false` returns `answer:null`. Summary: text, capped on a char boundary, labelled `source: model`. Quotes from the harvest block cannot verify (they are not in the markdown), by design. Offsets are char offsets in the model-visible text, which can be a truncated prefix (`truncated_input:true`).

### DEC-9. API shape, partial failure, dynamic support set (DECISION, extends D5 D8)

Typed objects in `formats[]` (D5 reserved the object form for this item): `{"type":"json","schema":{...},"prompt":"..."}`, `{"type":"summary","max_chars":1200,"language":"nb"}`, `{"type":"question","question":"..."}`, `{"type":"highlights","query":"...","max_highlights":5,"max_chars_each":400}`; at most one of each type per request (cost is bounded by formats times pages). Response fields on `data` (Firecrawl-shaped names): `json`, `summary`, `answer`, `highlights`, plus `ai: { json: {status, model, schema_valid, schema_issues, repair: "none"|"local"|"model", prefilled:[...], truncated_input, usage:{input_tokens, output_tokens, cost_usd?}}, ... }`. A failed model hop does **not** fail the scrape: the page and `markdown` return with `ai.<fmt>.status:"error"` and a typed `code` (`MODEL_UNAVAILABLE`, `ZDR_UNAVAILABLE`, `MODEL_RATE_LIMITED`, `SCHEMA_INVALID`, `BUDGET_EXHAUSTED`, `DISABLED`); request-level errors are only field errors from D5's validator (`400 error.details.fields[]`, so a Model that proposed a bad schema can read the issue and retry). D5 rejects `json|summary|question|highlights` with `unsupported_format` today: this item makes the `supported` list in that error **dynamic** (it contains the AI types only when `state.model_plane.ai_formats` is on). Scope: a service principal may request an AI format only with a new scope `extract:ai` (checked in the handler; no registry principal holds it today), so a Model-driven `fetch_url` or `web_fetch` can never make Quarry spend model money by adding `formats`; user and API-key tokens need none (entitlement and budgets govern).

### DEC-10. Cost control: budgets, the crawl multiplier, and what Quarry does and does not count (DECISION, tied to F-t2-008 and D6)

Quarry keeps **guards, not a ledger** (matrix rule 16). `AiBudget`, resolved per request through D6's `LimitsResolver` when it exists and from static floors before: `max_input_chars` per call (default 60_000, ceiling 80_000, the `ExtractStructured` cap), `max_output_tokens` per format (json 2048, summary 1024, question 512, highlights 1024; ceiling 8192), `max_calls` per request (default 8, equal to formats times pages), `max_total_tokens` per request (default 200_000, token counts from the response `usage`, estimated from characters over four when absent), `max_cost_usd` per request (enforced only when the gateway returns `cost_usd`, which it cannot under ZDR). On exhaustion mid-request, remaining pages return `ai.<fmt>.status:"skipped", code:"BUDGET_EXHAUSTED"` with D6's `dimension` (`llm_tokens`, `llm_calls`, `llm_cost`) and the request still returns its pages; `402 BUDGET_EXHAUSTED` only when the budget is spent before the first call. Concurrency for `/v1/extract` and multi-page work is bounded (default 4 per request, 16 per edge process) instead of the sequential loop. Metering (flag `QUARRY_EDGE__USAGE_LLM_METRICS=off|shadow|on`, default off): one event `quarry.extract.llm` per successful model-backed format with closed attributes (`kind`, `repair`, `zdr`, input and output token counts, no content), content-free under ZDR, id `quarry:<request_id>:<url_hash16>:<kind>`; this is a request to D6's DEC-10 vocabulary (its `llm_format` attribute already anticipates it). The **ledger of record** is cost-core, fed by the gateway (slice 1); under ZDR the Model Plane records nothing by doctrine (`grpc.rs:521-523`), so ZDR extraction cost is visible only as Quarry's own content-free counters. Crawl and batch multiply spend by pages: slice 9 gives each job an LLM token budget carried in the job params and decremented by the workflow from per-page results, and a page whose AI hop is skipped still crawls. Pricing (Firecrawl charges four credits per page per format) is a product decision, not made here (section 9).

### DEC-11. ZDR propagation for the lane (DECISION; lifts D4 row 23 conditionally)

The ZDR flag is passed on every call: `zdr:true` in the chat body whenever Quarry's effective ZDR is on (`claims.effective_zdr_mode(req.zdr)`), and the token is `tenant`. Model Plane answers with its typed refusals when no attested provider can serve: Quarry maps HTTP 412 and the body catalogue code (`zdr_unavailable`, `zdr_feature_not_supported` with feature `structured_output`) to `ai.<fmt>.status:"error", code:"ZDR_UNAVAILABLE", details.reason:<catalogue code>`, never a retry and never a silent non-ZDR fallback. Under ZDR Quarry persists nothing: no artifact, no scrape record, no page-cache entry (D5 rules), no extraction cache, no event content, no log of schema, prompt, page or output (names, kinds and counts only). D4 row 23 ("AI formats refused until the invoke request carries ZDR") is satisfied by sending the flag and honouring the typed refusal; if the owner prefers "refused for ZDR orgs until proven", the flag `QUARRY_EDGE__AI_FORMATS_ZDR=refuse|allow` (default `refuse` until the E2 proof passes, then `allow`) implements it without code change. Durable features (crawl, batch, schedules) stay refused under ZDR (existing `refuse_durable_job_under_zdr`), so slice 9 never sees a ZDR job.

### DEC-12. `/v1/extract` becomes a facade over one engine (DECISION)

One engine (`ai_extract`) serves `POST /v1/scrape` formats and `POST /v1/extract`. `/v1/extract` keeps its fetch (`fetch_page`, driver-direct) for now, gains `prompt` (no longer dead), `schema` through the same engine, bounded concurrency and per-item budgets, and its `data`/`structured` shape is unchanged for existing callers. Moving it onto `PageRunner` (robots, sniff, redaction) is D5 Epic E1 / F-t2-040 and out of scope. Deprecation signalling (Firecrawl deprecates `/extract`) is not added until the scrape path is live.

### DEC-13. Model selection (DECISION)

Routing aliases only, never vendor ids: `QUARRY_EDGE__AI_FORMATS_MODEL_JSON` default `verevon-balance` for `Simple`, `QUARRY_EDGE__AI_FORMATS_MODEL_JSON_COMPLEX` default the same alias until cost data justify `verevon-genius`; `QUARRY_EDGE__AI_FORMATS_MODEL_TEXT` default `verevon-budget` for summary, question, highlights and the repair call. The aliases exist in inference-core (`provider/fallback.rs:1008` doc). The caller cannot name a model.

### DEC-14. Interim Model-proposed route that needs no Quarry grant (DECISION; owner sibling (b))

A new shared module `MP/model-gateway/src/web_extraction.rs` [NEW] holds the logic of `ExtractStructured` (scrape through `state.quarry`, coerce through inference-core) with the fixes of section 2.2: schema text embedded in the prompt, validation plus one repair pass, `urls[]` (at most 5, bounded concurrency 3), a `mode` of `json|summary|question|highlights` reusing the same verbatim rules, a usage envelope (non-ZDR), content-free refusal under ZDR where no provider attests. It is exposed two ways: the gRPC `ExtractStructured` (additive proto fields) and a chat tool `extract_structured` in `tool_loop.rs` (web class, next to `fetch_url`, gated like it). "Model proposes, Quarry executes": the Model proposes the schema and URL, model-gateway runs the inference step it already owns, Quarry only fetches (inline, D5 slice 7). It serves chat and agents today and is the fallback if the grant is deferred; it does **not** serve the Quarry REST API, schedules, or crawls. Not recommended as the only route: it duplicates the engine in a second language and workspace; the shared corpus (slice 8) is the guard.

### DEC-15. Retire what the plan replaces (DECISION)

Delete `Q/quarry-runtime/src/structured_extract.rs` and the `StructuredExtractRequest/Response` types (`contracts.rs:941-957`) once slice 6 lands, following the rerank ruling ("deleted, not repaired"): they point at a phantom route and invite a second client. `ai_formats.rs` keeps `summary`/`query` for the answer pipeline (`answer.rs`, `answer_routes.rs`) unchanged and becomes a thin delegate for `json`. Correct `MPX/go/pkg/quarry/client.go`'s stale header (D5 recommends deleting it separately).

### DEC-16. Rate limit and metering classes in model-gateway (DECISION, slice 1)

`rate_limit.rs` keys one bucket per `org_id` (24, 78-118). Service principals get their own bucket `org|svc:<service_id>` with rpm `MODEL_GATEWAY_SERVICE_RPM` (default 120), so a crawl cannot starve chat. `ai_chat` publishes a `USAGE_ENVELOPE` for service callers only (`MODEL_GATEWAY_AI_CHAT_METERING=off|service|all`, default `service`; `all` would change user accounting and is out of scope), ZDR publishes nothing. `usage.cost_usd` is priced with `state.pricing.cost_usd_for(...)` as `invoke` does (9342-9362).

### DEC-17. Hermetic test strategy: the fake applies the real rules (DECISION)

The 2026-10-03 and 2026-10-06 ledger entries record that permissive doubles hid six defects. So the fake model-gateway (`Q/quarry-runtime/tests/support/fake_model_gateway.rs` [NEW]) **reads a golden contract instead of hard-coding behaviour**: `MPX/api/contracts/model-gateway-service-routes.json` [NEW] (rows: service, method, route, scope; plus the `x-inference-authorization` requirement and the two-scope rule) generated from the code table and compared by a Model Plane test, vendored into Quarry's fixtures with a SHA256SUMS pin, registered in `zdr-contract-sync.test.sh`. The fake therefore answers 403 for any route not in the table and for a missing second bearer, exactly like `authorize_principal_route` and `verify_delegated_service_inference_bearer`. Evidence comes in two tiers: **E1** (dev bypass, real model-gateway, no grant) proves the engine; **E2** (real Auth Core mint of both tokens, real gateway, real inference-core) is the only tier that counts as the deployment claim for the grant.
