## 8. What Verevon already does well (protect it)

Verevon is behind on the acquisition contract and the shared browser shell, but ahead of every reference on egress containment, governed execution, evidence and ZDR strictness, and it is the only system in this audit that combines a crawl engine with an interactive agent browser. [SRC]

- **Search.** The multi-provider router (Tantivy, Stract, SearXNG, Brave, Serper and Data Plane vectors) with hybrid rerank and ZDR gating beats every reference; paid providers are never used under ZDR (apps/Ingestion Plane/Quarry-v2/crates/quarry-edge/src/search_routes.rs:50-160; apps/Ingestion Plane/Quarry-v2/crates/quarry-runtime/src/smart_router.rs:39-60). [SRC] Do not regress: keep the org-keyed `SearchCache` (apps/Ingestion Plane/Quarry-v2/crates/quarry-edge/src/cache.rs:108-125) and fix only the gateway cache in front of it ([F-t2-003](#F-t2-003)).
- **SSRF and egress containment.** DNS is resolved once and pinned, redirects are re-validated per hop, each browser session gets a loopback DNS-pinned egress proxy with QUIC disabled, and a CDP Fetch guard denies by default (apps/Ingestion Plane/Quarry-v2/crates/quarry-runtime/src/dns_guard.rs:104; apps/Ingestion Plane/Quarry-v2/crates/quarry-runtime/src/fetch.rs:193-217; apps/Ingestion Plane/Quarry-v2/crates/quarry-browser/src/chromiumoxide.rs:186-243, 706-775). [SRC] Do not regress: extend it to tenant webhooks ([F-t2-031](#F-t2-031)) and require the same proof from remote providers ([F-t2-059](#F-t2-059)).
- **Governed execution.** One action per `/step`; the broker grant is validated before every action, broker domains replace any client list, domains are checked before and after each action, and agent runs are refused unless the driver proves isolated egress (apps/Ingestion Plane/Quarry-v2/crates/quarry-edge/src/agent_routes.rs:2257-2266, 1397-1412, 2294-2400, 1131-1141). [SRC] Do not regress: the grant redesign across [F-t2-001](#F-t2-001), [F-t2-011](#F-t2-011), [F-t2-013](#F-t2-013) and [F-t2-032](#F-t2-032) must keep "the Model proposes, Quarry executes or rejects".
- **One-shot sensitive approvals.** Dialog, upload and download actions need a fresh single-use grant scoped to the exact frame, dialog or artifact, and dialogs are never auto-answered (apps/Ingestion Plane/Quarry-v2/crates/quarry-edge/src/agent_routes.rs:135-312; apps/Model Plane/go/services/browser-broker/internal/grant/grant.go:32-36, 104-125). [SRC] Stricter than Aside's auto-accept. Do not regress: [F-t2-024](#F-t2-024) and [F-t2-039](#F-t2-039) must reuse this machinery.
- **Risk gates enforced in code.** Login, checkout, posting, destructive, cross-domain and persistent-cookie risks combine model self-report with a deterministic backstop and fail closed; web tool results are classified and screened unconditionally; the gateway denies `evaluate` for UI sessions (apps/Model Plane/rust/services/execution-core/src/browser_agent/browser_risk.rs; apps/Model Plane/rust/services/execution-core/src/provenance.rs:1-60; apps/Frontend Plane/verevonv3/apps/gateway/src/domains/browser.rs:3751-3755). [SRC] Do not regress: keep enforcement in code when adding per-origin consent ([F-t2-032](#F-t2-032)).
- **Evidence.** Immutable actor-scoped step receipts, checkpoints, proof bundles, a cursor timeline, an event stream with `from_seq` replay, and procedure compile with replay, quality and impact checks (apps/Ingestion Plane/Quarry-v2/crates/quarry-runtime/src/step_receipts.rs:183-560; apps/Ingestion Plane/Quarry-v2/crates/quarry-runtime/src/browser_procedure.rs:61; apps/Ingestion Plane/Quarry-v2/crates/quarry-edge/src/agent_routes.rs:2466-2538, 2658). [SRC] No reference matches this. Do not regress: surface it ([F-t2-014](#F-t2-014)) and add a store ([F-t2-051](#F-t2-051)) rather than rebuild it.
- **ZDR strictness.** Durable crawl and batch are refused, persistent profiles and resume are refused, events reach live subscribers only, artifacts are withheld, and model-gateway refuses browser runs under issuer-enforced ZDR (apps/Ingestion Plane/Quarry-v2/crates/quarry-edge/src/routes.rs:575-588; apps/Ingestion Plane/Quarry-v2/crates/quarry-edge/src/agent_routes.rs:527-534, 1347-1353; apps/Model Plane/rust/services/model-gateway/src/browser_run.rs:307-315; apps/Frontend Plane/verevonv3/apps/gateway/src/domains/browser.rs:3846-3870). [SRC] Stricter than any reference's incognito mode. Do not regress: check every new cache, monitor, inline payload or telemetry path against the ZDR matrix ([F-t2-003](#F-t2-003)).
- **Observation and locators.** Refs are bound to native CDP backend node ids, actions verify and act atomically, stale or ambiguous targets fail closed with `TARGET_REPAIR_REQUIRED`, semantic locators and frame refs cover cross-process frames, and console, network and lifecycle collectors run at CDP level so pages cannot tamper with them (apps/Ingestion Plane/Quarry-v2/crates/quarry-browser/src/chromiumoxide.rs:1086-1200, 1232, 1446-1610; apps/Ingestion Plane/Quarry-v2/crates/quarry-runtime/src/observation.rs:1019; apps/Ingestion Plane/Quarry-v2/crates/quarry-core/src/contracts.rs:640-827). [SRC] Do not regress: [F-t2-015](#F-t2-015) and [F-t2-034](#F-t2-034) add checks on this contract, not a second vocabulary.
- **Politeness and durable orchestration.** A per-host AIMD scheduler and a global autoscaled pool are wired into every page fetch; a Postgres SKIP LOCKED queue plus Temporal workflows give durability with deterministic job idempotency (apps/Ingestion Plane/Quarry-v2/crates/quarry-runtime/src/pipeline.rs:330-400; apps/Ingestion Plane/Quarry-v2/crates/quarry-runtime/src/postgres_queue.rs:74). [SRC] Do not regress: no second queue store; feed robots Crawl-delay into the existing scheduler ([F-t2-016](#F-t2-016)).
- **Webhook delivery and error envelope.** Delivery is HMAC-signed with backoff from 1 s to 1 h, five attempts and a dead-letter queue; the error envelope carries retryable codes, `retry_after_seconds` and `next_actions` (apps/Ingestion Plane/Quarry-v2/services/quarry-control/internal/dispatcher/dispatcher.go:25-34; apps/Ingestion Plane/Quarry-v2/crates/quarry-edge/src/api_error.rs:11-26; apps/Ingestion Plane/Quarry-v2/crates/quarry-core/src/error.rs:10-50). [SRC] Firecrawl's in-process webhook path shows no retry. [SRC] Do not regress: [F-t2-031](#F-t2-031) wraps the dispatcher with org scoping instead of replacing it.
- **Production defaults.** Production refuses to start with ephemeral stores or a default control key, Chromium is pinned, and internal control calls use HMAC rather than path secrets (apps/Ingestion Plane/Quarry-v2/crates/quarry-edge/src/main.rs:690-704; apps/Ingestion Plane/Quarry-v2/services/quarry-control/cmd/control/main.go:63-117). [SRC] Firecrawl's admin surface uses a secret in the URL path. [SRC] Do not regress: never adopt a secret-in-path gate.
- **Integration Plane.** integration-corev2 holds an AES-GCM token vault, a short-lived internal token broker, about 142 named provider operations with signed write attestations and mandatory idempotency keys, and a raw provider proxy that returns 410 (apps/Ingestion Plane/integration-corev2/internal/crypto/vault.go:16-20; apps/Ingestion Plane/integration-corev2/internal/actions/service.go:164; apps/Ingestion Plane/integration-corev2/internal/api/legacy_routes.go:69-74). [SRC] This is the shape Apify documents for its MCP connectors. [DOC] Do not regress: [F-t2-024](#F-t2-024) keeps secrets in the vault and lets Quarry resolve references only.
- **MCP client hardening and provisioning.** Outbound MCP connections use no redirects, DNS pinning per dial, SSRF re-validation of discovery and token hops and a mandatory exact tool allowlist; provisioning is standards-based SCIM through Control Plane principals. [SRC] Do not regress: an MCP server ([F-t2-064](#F-t2-064)) must reuse the same allowlist discipline.
- **Browser profiles with governance.** Profiles have scopes, a restore probe and an always-visible ZDR chip, and ZDR forbids a persistent profile at run creation on both the gateway and Quarry sides (agent_routes.rs:527-533), although Quarry's `/v1/profiles` routes themselves check org id only, with no ZDR or owner check, and `GET` returns raw cookie values ([F-t2-020](#F-t2-020)) (apps/Ingestion Plane/Quarry-v2/crates/quarry-edge/src/profile_routes.rs:102-432; apps/Frontend Plane/verevonv3/apps/gateway/src/domains/browser.rs:147-185). [SRC] Do not regress: keep scope separation and never widen an `org_shared` profile through a Space; [F-t2-020](#F-t2-020) adds encryption under the same store.
- **Bulk plus interactive.** Crawl, map, batch and schedules sit beside the interactive agent browser, which neither Codex nor Aside has. [SRC] Do not regress: keep bulk acquisition out of the agent's tab loop and offer it to the Model as approval-gated jobs ([F-t2-042](#F-t2-042)).
- **Takeover authority, with one correction.** Human input switches the session to takeover and agent actions get 409, but that check lives in the gateway (apps/Frontend Plane/verevonv3/apps/gateway/src/domains/browser.rs:663-759); Quarry's `/step` never reads `control_mode` (apps/Ingestion Plane/Quarry-v2/crates/quarry-edge/src/agent_routes.rs:2227-2294). [SRC] Protect the gateway check until phase 1 of [F-t2-013](#F-t2-013) moves enforcement into Quarry, then keep it there.
